# Track 2 Visualization Suite (`analyses/paradigm/figures/`)

Publication-grade figure suite for Study 3 Track 2 (**Morphological Paradigm Predictability / *i-complexity***), harmonized with Track 1 (`analyses/grambank/figures/`): `6.5 in` final width, Helvetica typography (`>= 7.0 pt` floor at final size), `300 DPI` PNG + vector PDF masters, consistent double-encoded semantic palettes reserving `#1c5cab` (deep blue), `#d95f26` (burnt orange), and `#8e1f5f` (magenta) for Track 1 **Enumerative breadth**, **Irregularity**, and **Contextual NP agreement** while encoding Track 2 POS consistently (**Verbs**: `#0d7a68` pine-teal / circle / solid; **Nouns**: `#9e6b06` ochre-bronze / triangle / dashed), exact exported posterior `80%` (`[q10, q90]`) and `95%` (`[q025, q975]`) credible intervals, and greyscale / CVD (Machado et al. 2009) / blur QC verification.

## 2. Figure Captions & Alt Text

### Figure 1 (`fig1_mgl_map_and_complexity.{png,pdf}`)
**Integrative paradigm entropy stays low across large synthetic paradigms in 56 GeLaTo languages.**  
**a**, Geographic distribution of the `56` GeLaTo-linked languages (`53` with language-level cross-family `f3_fixed` at `n_A >= 3`; `3` without `f3_fixed` shown as open markers; `94` orthographic paradigm units: `45` Verb, `49` Noun; `12` Glottolog v5.3 families). Marker fill encodes back-transformed Stage-1 cross-family `f3_fixed` (`x 10^-3`, square-root scale) and marker shape encodes POS coverage (`38` Verbs & Nouns, `11` Nouns only, `7` Verbs only). **b**, Distinct synthetic paradigm size (`n_cells_distinct`, log scale, up to `256` cells) versus cross-validated Vowel-Tier MGL tree conditional cross-entropy (`h_tree_bits` ± 1 CV SE) across all `94` orthographic units (`Verbs` pine-teal circles, `Nouns` ochre-bronze triangles). **c**, Empirical PCFP conditional-entropy drop across predictor contexts (`k = 1` single-cell baseline -> `k = 3` Product-of-Experts -> `Tree` Chu–Liu–Edmonds optimal branching); faint grey lines show individual units and thick trajectories show POS medians with IQR bars (overall median `2.17 -> 1.20 -> 0.58` bits; Verbs `0.54`, Nouns `0.72` bits), confirming Ackerman & Malouf's Low Conditional Entropy Conjecture.  
*Alt text:* Three-panel figure showing a world map of 56 languages colored by cross-family genetic admixture f3 (with 3 open markers for languages without f3), a scatter plot of paradigm cell count versus MGL tree entropy showing low entropy even at 100–256 cells, and a slopegraph showing conditional entropy dropping from 2.17 bits (1 predictor) to 0.58 bits (optimal tree).

### Figure 2 (`fig2_mgl_validation.{png,pdf}`)
**Multi-Locus Vowel-Tier MGL converges across harmony pre-abstraction, sample size, frequency stratum, orthography/IPA, and Track 1 Grambank domains.**  
**a**, Built-in Vowel-Tier MGL versus Malouf (2017) spectral vowel-harmony archiphoneme pre-abstraction across `33` Uralic, Turkic, and Mongolic-Khitan units (`r = 0.992` on `29` orthographic units, `r = 0.988` overall; dashed line marks 1:1 parity). **b**, Stability against `uniform120` baseline for `uniform480` (`n = 73` Tier-2 units, `r = 0.937`; `90.4%` lower or equal at `N_train = 480`) and `topfreq120` (`n = 74` units, `r = 0.980`; `70.3%` higher `H_tree` on frequent core lemmas). **c**, Paired orthographic versus Gold phonemic IPA (`12` Paralex/CELEX pairs), pinpointing orthographic neutralization in Russian unstressed vowel reduction and Estonian Q2/Q3 quantity alongside English opaque verbal spelling. **d**, Cross-track construct validity against Track 1 Grambank clean domains: top subpanel relates log distinct paradigm cells (`log |C|`) to POS-matched Track-1 enumerative breadth (`Nouns`: `n = 38, r = 0.61, rho = 0.50, PGLS beta = 0.54`; `Verbs`: `n = 35, r = 0.40, rho = 0.36, PGLS beta = 0.09`); bottom subpanel relates nominal `H_tree` to NA-masked Grambank nominal irregularity share (`n = 34, r = 0.45, p = 0.0077, rho = 0.44, PGLS beta = -0.18`) and 2PL latent nominal irregularity (`theta_n_irreg_eap`, `n = 34, r = 0.51, p = 0.0022, rho = 0.48`), with OLS fits and 95% CIs.  
*Alt text:* Four-panel validation figure showing 1:1 agreement between Vowel-Tier MGL and Malouf harmony abstraction, consistent shifts for 480-lemma and top-frequency splits, expected orthography-versus-IPA offsets, and positive cross-track correlations with Track 1 Grambank enumerative breadth and nominal irregularity.

### Figure 3 (`fig3_mgl_admixture_synthesis.{png,pdf}`)
**Cross-family admixture `f3_fixed` shows no systematic erosion of integrative paradigm predictability across Verbs and Nouns.**  
**a**, Standardized posterior slopes per SD of cross-family `f3_fixed` with exact `80%` (`[q10, q90]`, thick) and `95%` (`[q025, q975]`, thin) CrIs across Track 1 Grambank 2PL IRT-SEM maximal benchmarks (`Verb breadth`, `Noun breadth`, `Verb irregularity`, `Noun irregularity`, `Contextual NP agreement`) and Track 2 MGL Paradigm Predictability (`Verbs n = 42`, `Nouns n = 47`) across Block 1 (`Maximal [Phylo + GP]`, 4 locked complete-case LOO ablations, `Within-family [Mundlak]`) and 10 Block 2 sensitivity checks (`Full sample`, `Cutoff n >= 5`, `Strict links`, `Bouckaert stem`, `Bouckaert crown`, `Drop Inner Asia`, `Uniform-480`, `Top-freq-120`, `20-draw Berkson`, `Classical ME`). Shaded band marks the `[-0.15, +0.15]` SD ROPE. Under the primary maximal model (`Phylo + GP`), Verbs yield `b = -0.157` (`80% [-0.277, -0.025]`, `95% [-0.333, +0.072]`, `BF01 = 1.51`, `P(|b| <= 0.15) = 43%`) and Nouns yield `b = +0.085` (`80% [-0.124, +0.293]`, `95% [-0.231, +0.412]`, `BF01 = 2.66`, `P(|b| <= 0.15) = 58%`). **b**, Full specification curve sorting all `27` Verb and `26` Noun fitted `H_tree` models by posterior mean; `26/27` Verb (`median ROPE = 43%`) and `26/26` Noun (`median ROPE = 56%`) `95%` CrIs include zero.  
*Alt text:* Two-panel synthesis figure displaying Track 1 Grambank reference slopes alongside Track 2 Verb and Noun cross-family f3 slopes across 16 Block 1 and Block 2 specifications and full specification curves of 27 Verb and 26 Noun models, nearly all spanning zero.

### Figure 4 (`fig4_mgl_family_slopes.{png,pdf}`)
**Within-family `f3_fixed` trajectories remain flat across multi-language lineages.**  
**a**, Comparison of `Maximal (Phylo + GP)`, `Within-family (Mundlak)`, `Between-family (Mundlak)`, `Drop Inner Asia`, `Strict links`, and `Cutoff n >= 5` cross-family `f3_fixed` posterior slopes (`80%` and `95%` CrIs; shaded `[-0.15, +0.15]` SD ROPE) for Verbs and Nouns. **b**, Observed MGL `H_tree` (± 1 CV SE, square-root scale) versus back-transformed language-level cross-family `f3_fixed` (`x 10^-3`, square-root scale) across the four lineages with `n >= 3` languages in both POS (`Indo-European 25 V / 23 N`, `Uralic 9 V / 9 N`, `Turkic 3 V / 7 N`, `Afro-Asiatic 3 V / 3 N`), overlaid with OLS trend lines (95% CI ribbons shown for `n >= 9`).  
*Alt text:* Two-panel plot showing Global, Mundlak within- and between-family, regional, link, and cutoff sensitivity slopes alongside small-multiple scatterplots of cross-family f3 versus MGL tree entropy across Indo-European, Uralic, Turkic, and Afro-Asiatic.

### Figure 5 (`fig5_mgl_frequency_entrenchment.{png,pdf}`)
**Within-language token frequency entrenches irregular lemmas universally, with decisive null interaction against cross-family `f3_fixed` across 45,844 lemmas.**  
**a**, Language-specific within-language frequency slopes (exact posterior `80%` and `95%` CrIs from `entrench_{V,N}__all_lemmas__f3_fixed_n3.rds`, or tracked fallback `entrenchment_fig5_summary.csv`) sorted by posterior mean across `42` Verb (`19,784` lemmas) and `44` Noun (`26,060` lemmas) languages, with population hyper-posterior at bottom (`Verbs: +0.095 [+0.050, +0.140] SD, BF10 = 322, 37/42 > 0`; `Nouns: +0.031 [+0.014, +0.048] SD, BF10 = 11, P(>0) = 0.9996, 42/44 > 0`). **b**, Empirical within-language-centered `Delta H_tree` (bits, mean ± SE across 5 frequency quintiles) stratified by median language `f3_fixed` (`Low f3` vs. `High f3`), overlaid with exact Bayesian hierarchical model predictions and 95% CrI ribbons showing parallel upward entrenchment slopes. **c**, Exact posterior densities and `80%`/`95%` CrIs for the cross-level `Cross-family f3 x Token Frequency` interaction `b_int`, sitting `100%` inside the `[-0.15, +0.15]` SD ROPE (`Verbs: -0.002 [-0.044, +0.041], BF01 = 22.4`; `Nouns: -0.002 [-0.019, +0.016], BF01 = 55.3`).  
*Alt text:* Three-panel figure showing positive within-language frequency-entrenchment slopes across 42 Verb and 44 Noun languages, parallel empirical and modeled entropy gains across frequency quintiles for low- and high-f3 languages, and interaction posteriors concentrated 100% inside the null ROPE.

### Figure 6 (`fig6_mgl_maximal_effects.{png,pdf}`)
**Paradigm economy and phylogenetic inheritance dominate integrative paradigm entropy in the maximal phylogenetic-spatial model.**  
**a**, Fixed-effect posteriors on standardized MGL `H_tree` (mean, thick `80%` CrI `[q10, q90]`, thin `95%` CrI `[q025, q975]`; dashed zero line and shaded `[-0.15, +0.15]` SD band) in the primary maximal model (`main, n_A >= 3`: `Verbs n = 42`, pine-teal circles; `Nouns n = 47`, ochre-bronze triangles). Standardized continuous slopes (per `1 SD`) show negative paradigm-economy scaling for distinct paradigm size `log |C|` (`Verbs: -0.362, 80% [-0.497, -0.234], 95% [-0.579, -0.167], P(<0) = 0.9995`; `Nouns: -0.194, 80% [-0.402, +0.017], 95% [-0.511, +0.129], P(<0) = 0.882`), opposing geographic-area tendencies (`Verbs: +0.260, 95% [+0.021, +0.503]`; `Nouns: -0.245, 95% [-0.633, +0.164]`), weak or null speaker-population (`Verbs: -0.215, 95% [-0.551, +0.069]`; `Nouns: -0.002, 95% [-0.478, +0.461]`) and available-lexicon size (`Verbs: -0.089`; `Nouns: +0.040`) slopes, and null cross-family `f3_fixed` slopes (`Verbs: -0.157, 95% [-0.333, +0.072]`; `Nouns: +0.085, 95% [-0.231, +0.412]`). Lexicon provenance offsets (`Paralex` and `UniMorph` relative to curated national reference lexicons: Morph-it!, PoliMorf, VESUM; SD units) span zero broadly in both POS (`Paralex: -0.116` V, `-0.010` N; `UniMorph: -0.180` V, `-0.071` N). **b**, Posterior variance decomposition (`0%–100%` share of per-draw total variance `V_tot = sd_lang^2 + sdgp^2 + sigma^2 + mean(se_y^2)`): verbal `H_tree` is dominated by `Phylogeny` (`78.2%`, `80% [43.3%, 97.8%]`, `95% [13.4%, 99.0%]`; mean `SD = 1.06`) over `Spatial GP` (`17.6%`, `80% [0.3%, 51.2%]`; mean `SD = 0.44`) and `Language residual` (`3.4%`, `80% [0.0%, 8.7%]`; mean `SD = 0.16`), whereas nominal `H_tree` divides across `Phylogeny` (`43.7%`, `80% [4.1%, 80.6%]`; mean `SD = 0.85`), `Language residual` (`36.5%`, `80% [5.4%, 75.2%]`; mean `SD = 0.68`), and `Spatial GP` (`19.1%`, `80% [1.0%, 44.2%]`; mean `SD = 0.52`), with cross-validation `CV measurement SE` contributing `< 1%` (`0.8%` V, `0.7%` N; RMS `SE = 0.10 SD`). **c**, Spatial GP squared-exponential correlation decay `rho(d)` across `0–6,000 km` great-circle distance (posterior median and `80%` CrI ribbons from `brms` `gp_exp_quad_cov` on `Xgp_1 = raw_xyz / dmax`, verified `dmax = 1.987` V, `1.911` N), showing continental macro-areal correlation with median half-correlation distance `d_0.5 = 3,049 km` (`80% [1,377, 7,072] km`) for Verbs (solid pine-teal) and `d_0.5 = 1,837 km` (`80% [952, 4,363] km`) for Nouns (dashed ochre-bronze).  
*Alt text:* Three-panel figure showing the anatomy of the maximal Verb (n = 42) and Noun (n = 47) MGL tree-entropy models: Panel a displays standardized fixed-effect slopes and lexicon-provenance offsets highlighting negative paradigm-size slopes (-0.36 Verbs, -0.19 Nouns) alongside null cross-family f3 slopes; Panel b shows percentage shares of total variance led by phylogeny (78% Verbs, 44% Nouns), spatial GP (18% Verbs, 19% Nouns), language residual (3% Verbs, 37% Nouns), and <1% CV measurement error; Panel c plots spatial GP correlation decay over 0 to 6,000 km with median half-correlation distances of 3,049 km for Verbs and 1,837 km for Nouns.
